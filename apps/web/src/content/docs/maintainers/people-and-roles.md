# People and roles

Everyone who can push to the repository on GitHub is a maintainer in Babblebird on their own; nobody has to add them. Maintainers manage the project on its manage page, reached from **Your projects** or the **Manage** button on the project.

## Who can translate

Under **Who can translate**, choose between:

- **Anyone signed in**: every account can translate. Good for most open source projects.
- **Only people you add**: everyone else can still suggest, and your translators use their suggestions.

## Roles

| Role       | Can                                                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Translator | Translate and use suggestions                           |
| Reviewer   | Everything a translator can, and approve                |
| Maintainer | Everything, including the project's settings and people |

Translators and reviewers can be limited to some languages and some areas, for example a reviewer for German in the desktop only.

## Adding people

Under **People**, choose **Manage people**, then **Add someone**, and enter their GitHub name or email address. If they don't have a Babblebird account yet, they're invited and join as soon as they sign in with that GitHub account or address.
